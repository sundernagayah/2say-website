2Say Films is built around a simple belief: images should do more than look beautiful. They should leave something behind.

01 Est. Singapore
01 Where we began

We began with the urge to make.

2Say grew from the kind of filmmaking where everyone gets their hands dirty — writing, producing, shooting, editing, solving, rebuilding. That closeness to the work still shapes how we make films today.

We move between narrative, music, commercial and original work, but the question stays the same: what is this image trying to make someone feel?

Founded by filmmaker Sunder Nagayah

Singapore-based. Hands-on by design. Built to stay flexible enough for small crews and ambitious enough for cinema.

02 What we believe
01

Human before format.

Genre, platform and technique matter. People matter more. Performance and emotional truth come first.

02

Craft should serve feeling.

Lighting, lenses, production design and rhythm are not decoration. Every choice should carry the story.

03

Small teams can build big worlds.

We value resourcefulness over excess — making scale through taste, preparation and strong collaborators.

04

Singapore, looking outward.

We are rooted here, shaped by many cultures, and interested in stories that can travel without losing where they came from.
